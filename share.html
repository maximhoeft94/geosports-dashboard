Why

GeoSports only shows your question-by-question scores to you. This one-click tool reads your own results for every day since October 2 and turns them into a code you text to Maxim. Your scores are only added if the five questions add up to your official daily score.

How to share (on a computer)

  1. Save the button. Show your bookmarks bar (Chrome or Edge: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B; Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar), then drag this button onto it:
    FVBK share code
  2. Open geosports.app and make sure you’re signed in.
  3. Click the “FVBK share code” bookmark while you’re on the GeoSports page. A box appears and collects your rounds.
  4. Press “Copy code” and text the code to Maxim. Do it about once a week. Each code covers the whole season, so a missed week loses nothing.

Make a new bookmark by hand, name it “FVBK share code”, and paste this whole line as the address:
